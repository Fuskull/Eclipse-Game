HOW IT WORKS:

3. Kill ALL enemies in the room
4. Door appears (green) when room is cleared
5. Touch door to progress to next room

📈 DIFFICULTY SCALING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FORMULA:
difficulty = 1 + (roomCount * 0.2)

WHAT SCALES:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. ENEMY HEALTH
   - Patrol: 50 + (difficulty - 1) * 20
   - Hunter: 100 + (roomCount - 1) * 30

2. ENEMY SPEED
   - Patrol: 100 * difficulty
   - Room 1: 100 speed
   - Room 5: 200 speed
   - Room 10: 300 speed

3. ENEMY COUNT
   - Patrol: 2 + roomCount (max 5)
   - Hunter: roomCount / 2 (max 3)

PROGRESSION CURVE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- 2 patrol enemies (50 HP each)
- Total: 100 HP to clear
- Easy

Room 3:
- 4 patrol enemies (90 HP each)
- 1 hunter (130 HP)
- Total: 490 HP to clear
- Moderate

💡 STRATEGY TIPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EARLY ROOMS (1-3):
- Enemies die quickly
- Gun is efficient
- Save ammo for later
- Learn enemy patterns

MID ROOMS (4-6):
- Enemies take multiple hits
- Multi-Shot becomes essential
- Use walls for cover
- Prioritize hunters (more XP)

LATE ROOMS (7-10):
- Enemies are bullet sponges
- MUST have upgrades
- Kite enemies around walls
- Focus fire one at a time
- Melee risky but efficient

UPGRADE PRIORITY:
1. Multi-Shot (CRITICAL for late game)
2. Faster Cooldowns (more DPS)
3. Ammo Capacity (don't run out)
4. Health Regen (sustain)
5. Max Health (survive longer)

COMBAT TACTICS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✓ Kite enemies around walls
✓ Focus one enemy at a time
✓ Use dash to reposition
✓ Collect pickups between fights
✓ Save melee for low-health enemies
✓ Don't get surrounded
✓ Watch ammo counter
✓ Prioritize hunters (they chase you)
