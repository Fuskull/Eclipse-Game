HOW TURRETS WORK:
----------------
1. Turret spawns at position
2. Shows "turret alive.png" sprite (1.2x scale)
3. Moves back and forth on horizontal line (±100 pixels)
4. Detects player within 300 pixel range
5. Shoots orange bullet toward player every 2 seconds
6. Bounces off walls and reverses direction
7. When health reaches 0:
   - Changes to "turret dead.png" sprite
   - Stops moving
   - Stops shooting
   - Becomes semi-transparent
   - Still counts as "dead enemy" for room clear
